Vertical / phone capture

Fills the frame with a full-screen phone compose, so the email becomes the whole screen — record the 9:16 frame directly, no cropping a small portion. Turning it on auto-sets a 9:16 (540×960) frame and enlarges the typing text. Toggle off to restore your previous size.

OBS present mode

For an OBS Browser Source: this copies a link that opens the phone full-screen with your current script baked in — controls hidden, auto-plays on load. Paste it as the Browser Source URL and set the source size to 540×960 (then scale it up to your 1080×1920 canvas in OBS). Refresh the source to replay for another take.
