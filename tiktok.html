In Word-timed, each script note is matched to a paragraph of words (silence-gap detection). Place/tags/trigger come from your script.
Markup cheatsheet
Per-note directives (one note = paragraph block, separated by blank lines):
[[image:1]] · [[video:2]] — drop media #N from the pool
[[place:top-half]] — placement preset (see Layout below)
[[place:x=10,y=20,w=80,h=40]] — custom % rectangle
[[tags:fyp,trend,2024]] — hashtags (# auto-prefixed)
[[trigger:end]] · [[trigger:2500]] · [[trigger:manual]] · [[trigger:typed]]
[[muted:false]] · [[loop:false]] · [[fit:contain]] · [[z:10]] · [[behind]]
[[exit:previous]] — remove the previous note when this one appears
[[exit:all]] — clear all prior notes

Inline (inside body text):
[[pause:1500]] · [[wrong|right]] typo

Top-level (anywhere):
[[hook:Trends, ranked]] — sticky big text at top
[[handle:@user|caption]] — TikTok handle + caption overlay
